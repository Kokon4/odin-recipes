Lasagna

Description

This recipe has been called the best by many and has thousands of 5-star reviews! It uses simple ingredients that you likely have on hand, and it packs big flavor! This is an easy lasagna recipe. It requires just one pan, one bowl, and a 9×13 baking dish. It reheats well and can be made ahead of time. You can freeze lasagna either before or after baking.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 can (28 oz) crushed tomatoes
  • 1 can (6 oz) tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 teaspoon dried basil
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
